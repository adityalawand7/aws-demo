A beginner's guide to specialty coffee brewing

What is Specialty Coffee?

Specialty coffee refers to the highest grade of coffee available, typically tracked from single-origin farms and roasted to highlight unique flavor profiles. Unlike commercial coffee, it focuses on quality, sustainability, and the distinct characteristics of the bean's origin.

Essential Brewing Methods

1. The Pour Over (V60 / Chemex)

Produces a clean, crisp cup of coffee. It highlights bright acidity and subtle tasting notes by filtering out most oil and sediment.

2. French Press

An immersion brewing method that delivers a rich, full-bodied flavor. Because it uses a metal mesh filter, the natural oils remain in the cup.

3. AeroPress

A highly versatile, rapid method using air pressure. Perfect for travel, it can create anything from a light filter style to a concentrated espresso-like brew.

The Golden Ratios

For the best balance, use a digital scale to weigh your coffee and water. Here is a standard 1:16 ratio guide:
